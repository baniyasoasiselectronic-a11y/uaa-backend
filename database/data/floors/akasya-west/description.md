Akasya West brings 41 one and two bedroom apartments to Warsan Fourth, International City (Phase 2), Dubai. Each home has its own balcony or terrace, and the building rises over seven floors of apartments.

## Project at a glance
- Total number of apartments: **41**
- Floors of apartments: **7** (1st to 7th floor)
- Total built-up area: **4,429.85 sq m** (47,682.51 sq ft)
- Apartment types: **1 BHK and 2 BHK**

## Facilities
- Separate ladies' and gents' gyms on the 1st floor
- A private balcony or terrace for every apartment, with large terraces on the 7th floor
- Two lifts and two staircases serving every floor
